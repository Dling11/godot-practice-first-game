# Style Guide

**Twinsteel complete carry variant (2026-10-12, Decision196):** A new gait may use a distinct whole-cel motion version with owner authorization; never alter the frozen reference or silently pass it through the exact-reuse gate. For steady carry, preserve the entire approved upper anatomy through the waist, fixed real palms and native density; only authored legs step. Match ready/run blade angles and source-derived cuff masks. Check actual idle/run/attack/rest transitions in moving native captures, connectivity/margins/soles, foot alternation and protected source hashes. Height alone, a stamped head or a static board is insufficient. [Current recipe/source](art_source/generated/characters/king/dual_carry_gait_2026_10_12/README.md).

**Reviewed gait grips (2026-10-12):** For an immutable complete walking library, author and retain each near/far palm's identity across the full cyclic gait. Ranking all brown components can pick a shoulder, belt or boot; color/connectivity alone do not prove a hand. Copy the complete native closed fist/cuff border into the foreground mask, keeping both blade scales and camera-relative depth. Inspect mirrored side and vertical motion, wraparound, actual input/travel, blocked travel and run-to-attack/idle. Keep the frozen body pixels exact; a sword attachment correction never requires resizing King. [Twinsteel correction](art_source/review/combat/king_dual_gait_2026_10_12/README.md).

**Mandatory complete King reference (2026-10-11, Decision195):** Future weapons start from immutable assets/characters/playable/king/body_library/v1 and [whole turnaround/gait board](art_source/reference/characters/king/body_library_v1/README.md). Reuse complete standing/walking bodies; never resize King for steel or accept head/height alone. Cell192×128, scale0.5 and origin(0,-16) remain fixed. For existing attack registration, translate the complete cel with its mask, head provenance and every blade grip/axis; never counter-shift the hips/head independently. Copy queued/released head rectangles exactly, without rebasing them a second time against the first cel. New anatomy needs a distinct reviewed version; hashes and complete-pixel gates protect accepted views. [Required workflow](docs/design/king-shared-body-library.md). native_identity_v1 is historical head provenance, not sole body authority.

**Rejected motion requires new motion (2026-10-07):** A proportion-only re-fit does not satisfy an explicit choreography redesign. Generate whole connected bodies with real turning/back-camera anatomy and effects painted in the appropriate ground perspective. Preserve native identity and source sleeve pixels beside head transplants; do not invent limbs. Use real transparent gutters and shared frame anchors instead of nominal sheet divisions when drawn effects cross the grid. Check grounded first contact, blade-to-impact alignment and complete source margins in native playback. Geometry/assertion passes do not establish owner feel approval. [Current example](docs/design/king-axe-cardinal-motion.md).

**Vertical proportion convention (2026-10-07):** Calibrate anatomical torso/head/sole landmarks against approved side art, with one density per source motion lane; pose extents change naturally during crouches/strides/turns. Head provenance rectangles can include sleeves, collar and transparent crown padding: isolate face/hair while retaining blue/neutral sleeve material and connected warm leather arms, rather than erasing the rectangle. Leather is not crimson cloth. Preserve original palm texels/coverage and metadata alongside resampled complete cels; source-occluded hands remain hidden. Verify complete-body connectivity and ready-to-attack native motion, not just valid resources. [Current example](docs/design/king-cardinal-proportions.md).

**Twinsteel ready/idle correction (2026-10-07):** Ready blades must separate from their actual authored palms without crossing the torso. Separate idle-entry keys from looping glance keys; settle at neutral for one normal glance exposure before the first turn, within the same cancellable tween. Optional entry-end data defaults to the existing last cel. Owner accepts current Twinsteel attacks/body but warns against progressively bulkier sprites: keep native head/body density, palette and outlines consistent; fix attachment/pose/timing without rescaling approved body art. [Current guide](docs/design/king-dual-v2-cardinal.md).

**Cardinal idle and raster-copy convention (2026-10-07):** Bake head turns into complete body cels over a fixed torso; author optional rest start/loop/retrieval data with defaults preserving existing families. Use timed callbacks for long stationary pose loops. Detached planted blades own ground landmarks, not folded palms; source-occluded blades require explicit visibility data. Duplicate raster image pixels before clearing/remapping a potentially shared source/target slot. Validate every alpha cell and actual queued/released/native-rendered FX bounds, not only metadata. [Twinsteel example](docs/design/king-dual-v2-cardinal.md).

**Native side-study ingestion (2026-10-07):** Preserve one density across all new body cels and the approved rigid steel/head palette. Check thin drawn forearm coverage after downsampling and restore original neutral sleeves erased beside the native head, without fabricating an arm or overwriting retained face pixels. Visible blades require drawn connected palms; a genuinely hidden far arm also hides its blade. Keep exact full-body/both-grip queue boundaries and independent released idle. A side-only presentation GIF does not prove cardinal gameplay or enemy balance. Record exact prompts, snapshot hashes and native captures. [Current example](docs/design/king-dual-side-redesign.md).

**Approved replacement retirement (2026-10-07):** When the owner ends a comparison, remove the retired selectable definition and preserve accepted replacement IDs. Migrate legacy ownership and equipped references together during validation/restoration; collapse duplicates and keep unrelated unknown/unowned validation strict. Archive obsolete definitions/tests with .gdignore and hashes, but retain shared source/art dependencies. Per-cut accepted-contact audio belongs in optional immutable data with existing fallbacks and one shared cue per cleave. Record controlled enemy checks separately from moving/cold/device measurements. Scythe silhouette selection precedes motion/equipment; a request for alternatives is not final design approval. [Example](docs/design/weapon-impact-closure.md).

**Rear-facing anatomy and source ownership (2026-10-07):** Front arms can be fully hidden by an authored rear torso; declare this occlusion explicitly and retain both real source palms in provenance. Do not demand visible foreground gloves through a character's back. Register the pair together on one rigid hilt, hide its foreground mask only in marked keys, and preserve entry visibility. Padding/largest-component checks alone cannot detect connected neighbors: use calibrated gaps where source silhouettes touch, and assign complete FX ribbons before fitting. Verify actual rendered depth and the reported small fragments. [Example and proof](docs/design/king-greatsword-front-combo.md).

**Atlas/key remapping (2026-10-07):** Resource.duplicate alone does not isolate mutable packed pose arrays; explicitly duplicate them before key reordering and verify rendered grips/queue endpoints in every facing. Isolate the actual connected body before get_used_rect/shared-density registration. Calibrate real FX gutters, use a common row fit and complete alpha margins, and enable AtlasTexture.filter_clip. Never enlarge King to sell reach. Preserve raw sources/prompts and reproducible offline builds.

**Held intent:** Begin only from accepted unhandled world input, reuse authoritative requests and own at most one automatic followup. Release clears only that intent. Catch paused/focus-loss release and UI/action boundaries; test full combo wraparound. Timer work runs only during an active hold.

**Two-hand/jump convention (2026-10-07):** Two-hand flags require two real palms connected through visible forearms on a complete cel and registered to one rigid hilt. Remove source-only colored guides offline; verify both palms/body/native head, physical handle spacing and one density across a motion sheet. Grounded jump contact must match the actual melee ACTIVE boundary. Register the canonical blade tip to the sole plane across landing keys; do not sell a cleave by burying a long vertical sword or by moving a separate head/arm at runtime. Use a padded raster release plus a fixed ground burst that finishes across recovery. Camera feedback has one owner. [Example](docs/design/king-greatsword-front-combo.md).

**Attack variant convention (2026-10-07):** Keep accepted steel, carry, gait and idle intact when replacing only attacks. Back up dependencies with hashes and preserve the original as a separately equipable item when the owner requests comparison. Preview-only work enters equipment only after explicit authorization and real contact/collision/buffer/cancel checks. Label reused vertical bodies as fallback art; do not describe them as newly authored facings. Historical GIFs/counts retain delivery-time scope and cannot establish current play or owner feel approval.

**Weapon Bag/audio conventions (2026-10-06):** Compose icons from approved raster steel with fixed nearest sampling, aspect preservation, complete padding and lossless/no-mipmap/no-alpha-border imports. A paired weapon must show both blades in its item icon. Label DPS as base: mean complete-chain damage divided by all authored phases, excluding defenses, crits, buffs, misses, hitstop and speed gear. Impact samples belong to accepted contacts, once per cleave; swings remain separate. Retain downloaded source, license, processing and hashes. Use optional defaults to preserve explicitly frozen families.

**Axe-head thrust and stable raster registration (2026-10-06):** Preserve native body/carry/gait pixels and complete reflected weapon bases. A head push must project a real pale cutting-edge texel along aim, with the butt behind; an arbitrary face/crown point or shaft direction alone is insufficient. Current Iron Beard landmark is(8,30), drawn grip(39,33), source shaft(4,10). A one-hand secondary anchor is orientation, not a floating second palm. Generated FX columns may violate nominal grids: use true transparent gutters so a third-tip fragment cannot enter frame four, align all leading tips, then use one union crop/scale. Retain complete alpha padding, lossless nearest imports and disabled alpha-border fixing. Verify real production poses, broad impact visibility, queue/release endpoints and contact misses separately.

**Match FX art to its real contact lane (2026-10-06):** A valid atlas and visible fifth slash do not establish first-hit readability. Review each authored attack against its own contact shape and rotated cardinal UV mapping. When changing a slash into a thrust, register that raster sequence into the narrower lane rather than letting clipping leave isolated debris. Use a shared transform across its ignition/contact/fade cels, preserve other rows byte-for-byte and retain contact authority. Body drawing sheets exclude FX; review real playback and actual Player captures for effect visibility.

**Head axis and raised-sleeve validation (2026-10-06):** Asymmetric crimson scarf pixels are not a reliable front head center. Preserve the drawn crown/neck axis and scarf anatomy when replacing a smaller native head; remove discarded scalp fragments without erasing genuine gloves or sleeves. Neutral charcoal sleeve/outline pixels are anatomy too, not just blue armor highlights. Restore from the actual source arm region while preserving the accepted face. A high whole-body connectivity percentage can hide a detached small fist: verify each raised glove connects to the torso. Pose-only export sheets must disclose excluded swing FX; verify reported missing FX in the real Player with native renders.

**Sketch ownership and readable pixel edges (2026-10-06):** Identify the actual near and far hands from the owner reference before assigning sword layers. A brown pixel can be a wrist or belt; grip distance and connectivity do not prove the right hand holds the right blade. Preserve the complete native closed palm, chest and face. An exposed near sword paints before the body but under its real forearm/finger mask; a hidden rear arm/handle paints behind the torso. Composite the complete foreground arm after the walking legs rather than slicing it at their seam. Keep charcoal edges separating glove, sleeve, guard and vest at actual0.5 scale, using the native palette. Offline perimeter work must start from immutable pixels on every build, retain the same canvas/grip/tip and apply consistently across states. Review native mirrored gait and run-to-attack motion; automated passes never constitute owner visual approval.

**Occluded carry and identity references (2026-10-06):** A handle hidden by the far-side body should not protrude behind the invisible hand. A carry-only variant may erase those handle pixels while preserving the canonical canvas, tip, blade, guard, grip registration and depth; retain a complete near sword. Keep nearest/lossless/no-mipmap imports and disable alpha-border repair for both blade variants. Compare the actual running face against the independently approved standing identity, not merely against a rejected run source. Register replacements at the scarf socket and inspect neck continuity across all stride keys. Authored entry must inherit blade textures as well as rig transforms/offsets/depth, then restore the destination clip textures on its next cel. Verify this in the actual Player, including both mirrored side runs.

**Native gait anatomy:** When idle and running reuse the same native facing, preserve a complete head/neck/scarf/chest cel rather than applying head attachment again. Match the idle registration, move upper anatomy and glove landmarks together, and retain original leg keys at the pelvis seam. A head-size envelope and consistency within the run can pass while every run head is displaced relative to idle; compare against the independently accepted native body as well. When holding the first upper cel steady, register every source pelvis/leg key beneath that same center; original whole-body lateral sway cannot be copied unadjusted into the lower body. Preserve the alternating stride and native silhouettes, then inspect both pelvis rows and boot lanes across all keys at native size.

**Held weapon registration (2026-10-05):** Repose carrying forearms around their drawn shoulder attachments when the fists are too far forward; retain native head/neck/torso density and palette. Upper anatomy, both grips and finger masks must share one whole-pixel bob. Keep the leg cycle and hip seam coherent; cardinal upper bodies need not alternate sideways with the legs. Resize a canonical blade only from an immutable original and update its grip/tip in every pose; repeated builds must not compound resizing. Visible weapon length and broad contact FX may differ deliberately, while gameplay reach remains authoritative. Verify actual glove attachment, preserved native anatomy, non-crossing small tilt, planted boots and native-size motion. Ignore RGB differences only where both compared texels are fully transparent. Technical checks do not substitute for owner visual approval. For authored attack entry, inherit each blade sprite local offset together with its rig transform and body/hand/depth, because carrying and attacking can use different raster grip landmarks. Reset those offsets to the current clip registration after the first inherited cel; never compensate by moving the drawn glove. Both swords share the same pose clock.

**Watched animation outputs:** Finish GIF frame timing/loop metadata in a staging `.tmp` file, then replace the final artifact once and decode-check it. Repeated writes to a published GIF can race native importers or media previews holding a mapped section. The shared GIF helper supports Windows PowerShell5.1 null-backup replacement through `[NullString]::Value`.

**Dual-weapon convention (2026-10-05):** secondary_hands orients the primary shaft; give a second blade an explicit optional offhand resource with its own grip/tip/axis/visibility/depth for every cel. Both blades use one complete-body pose clock and foreground fingers. Preserve both transforms/depth and the whole body at queued boundaries. Do not rotate a two-glove overlay about only one hilt; current dual clips use crisp authored keys. Turn the complete body for spins, advance gait by actual travel, and retain fixed blade scale. Generate FX with generous cell gutters, preserve raw rejected sources and use one frame density per row so ignition/fade are not enlarged independently. One contact per input remains combat authority regardless of visible blade count.

**Small pose centering (2026-10-05):** When reducing a drawn torso lean, move head/neck/shoulders together and taper toward planted boots. Use lossless integer row translation offline, retain original head/palette and transform the foreground hand mask consistently before locating the final sword grip. Keep corrections small; current up-only Greatsword reduction is50%, capped at4 logical texels. Validate connected anatomy, unchanged grounded rows and actual full-motion entry/queue/release. Avoid independently centering only the head or rotating the gameplay actor.

**Directional anatomy and FX consistency (2026-10-05):** Connected alpha and identical head pixels do not establish correct head/torso alignment. Inspect enlarged moving poses against the original drawn scalp and collar. For back-view attacks, reference hair only: center/bottom alignment must preserve the moving torso, rather than copying an idle scarf/shoulder patch or anchoring to a scarf tail. Keep ordinary slash atlas/scale/density consistent across facings; the owner rejected the smaller upward-only fit. Preserve blade-bound meteor art and authoritative contact shapes. Use native per-facing pixel coverage plus full body/weapon captures, not only source-byte assertions. [Current implementation](docs/design/king-greatsword-oathbound-motion.md).

**Grounded family motion (2026-10-05):** Register grounded complete bodies by boot soles rather than scarf centers. An explicit authored lift supplies airtime; do not double it with drifting body origins. Center vertical idle on its soles, retain original head pixels at actual neck sockets and preserve only real foreground gloves. A running head has no raised-crown hand mask; warm hair must not be mistaken for a glove. Keep raw art and original accepted assets; derive revised clips in a separate runtime folder with prompt/provenance and nearest/lossless import settings. Store phase walking factors on WeaponDefinition; keep forward steps/knockback/dodge independent. For upward FX, use sibling painter order at the actor's normal Z instead of putting the body above scenery or FX below terrain. [Implementation](docs/design/king-greatsword-oathbound-motion.md).

**Head/neck silhouette rule (2026-10-05):** Never clear an expanded rectangular halo around a transplanted head or infer correct anatomy from identical head bytes. Anchor the original silhouette to the current drawn neck, preserve transparent edges by alpha composition and retain real foreground hands. Hair bounds must exclude thin arm outlines; source/cel density stays fixed. Native connected head/torso/glove checks, crown coverage and enlarged all-facing image review complement source identity checks. Allow identity occlusion only where the same brown glove is present in the registered hand atlas. [Current implementation](docs/design/king-greatsword-oathbound-motion.md).

**Greatsword identity ingestion (2026-10-05):** Preserve raw raster sources and exact prompts; extract complete components across grid boundaries. Use one fixed nearest density per directional source and register the crimson scarf/neck rather than changing airborne bounds. Protect installed head RGBA, quantize the rest to the installed directional carry palette, then register final gloves. Oathbound PNG imports disable `process/fix_alpha_border` because it changes copied transparent edge RGB; nearest/no mipmaps remain. Native visual inspection supplements identity/grip assertions: pixel checks alone missed a duplicate back-view head during development. [Current motion](docs/design/king-greatsword-oathbound-motion.md).

**Whole-body weapon motion (2026-10-05):** Treat torso, arms, wrists, blade and stance as one authored pose. The owner rejects detached limbs circling steel over repeated torso cels even when geometric checks pass. Prefer complete installed cels and sharp, intentional exposure; do not infer heavy weight requires long holds. Optional queued recovery branches must own complete moving cels and finish at the next opening; released branches finish on carry. Standalone/late input inherits the actual rendered opening. Validate full-pose handoffs and native pacing against an accepted weapon, with owner feel separate from test counts.

**Directional motion convention (2026-10-05):** Validate complete optional attack/carry/run direction sets and retain established fallbacks when absent. Generate actual front/back bodies rather than rotating a side body. Use one source density, logical foot plane, canonical rigid weapon and visible-glove registration. Queue openings from the actual preceding contact-end body/grip; settle unqueued clips to shared carry. Positive complete pose_weights distribute existing phase time; frame count does not set duration. Interpolate weapon angle with grip pinned to the drawn cel and registered foreground glove; keep body cels crisp and existing default playback unchanged. Use actual travel for gait and explicit opt-outs for unauthored rest transitions. Native checks complement owner feel.

**Weapon promotion:** Share immutable approved shapes/timings/art between game and review. Add opt-in profiles, preserve saved IDs/defaults and use existing non-saving debug unlocks until acquisition is implemented. Actual travel advances gait; physics tweens/timers handle poses/rest. Finish observers settle before queued family dispatch. On recursive teardown, queue presentation children without removing siblings synchronously. Extract needed helpers before archiving unused tools, retain source/manifest hashes and .gdignore, then prove rebuild hashes and native equipped gameplay.

**Longer attack clips:** Declare pose_count plus complete matching arrays, contiguous phase windows and enough atlas rows. Existing clips default8. Verify actual contact endpoint→entry and last key→carry in both directions, not arbitrary recovery/guard keys. For a fifth-only repair, preserve canonical weapon and prior action assets, disclose generated patch regions/source omissions, and validate protected identity pixels. Rear cosmetic trails must remain phase-scoped presentation with no damage authority. Short timer-gap tests should observe transition signals and use a watchdog instead of unbounded polling.

**Name weapon paths explicitly:** When words such as “opposite” or “return” are ambiguous, specify the inherited finish pose, target side, facing, blade path and next finish. Do not infer a rear contact or facing reversal from those words. For a focused continuation preview, preserve approved prior keys and exact entry before judging newly drawn motion; numeric boundary equality still requires visual/owner review.

**Storyboard registration and scope (2026-10-04):** Generated sprite sheets may cross nominal cells even after a spacing edit. Register grounded foot height independently of slash bounds while leaving authored horizontal lean and explicit jumps intact. Report alpha/noise filtering and source padding in an audit. A native renderer displaying a storyboard is not proof of gameplay timing, hit authority, fixed weapon geometry or owner feel approval.

**Linked stroke continuity (2026-10-04):** Compare the last CONTACT pose with the next strike entry, not only the prior recovery/guard pose. **Character consistency (2026-10-04):** A reference-guided generated sheet can still change anatomy, clothing or palette; registration and contact tests do not prove an identity match. **Combo continuity (2026-10-04):** Do not reverse an entire anticipation/contact/recovery clip as a substitute for a return swing. **Side effects and reverse cuts (2026-10-04):** Test actual rendered atlas pixels on both horizontal sides; a mirrored hit polygon alone does not prove visible FX. Mirror ordinary sample X, or reflect both axes of an explicit registered basis including handedness, never both paths at once. **Grip and reusable stance conventions (2026-10-04):** An opaque pixel is insufficient proof of a grip: register the glove center and inspect the visible hilt at native scale. Held weapon can draw above torso, with only the glove mask above the weapon; reserve behind-body whole-weapon ordering for stowed/released poses. Back carry needs rear shoulder/shaft attachment and its own handedness, not the attack's cutting-edge reflection. Taller blades need hand-aligned preparation before release; body-only lift remains presentation. Verify at least two unequal blade lengths and both horizontal facings before claiming reusable geometry; each new artwork still needs visual fit approval.

**Gait and burial registration (2026-10-04):** Register moving bodies to a stable torso/head landmark; do not independently rebase X to whichever foot is lowest. Inspect all consecutive contact/down/passing/flight keys, not only unique hashes or every-other-frame galleries. Held shoulder/back anchors should track modest body bob without horizontal jitter. Locomotion presentation can consume measured travel; collision must stop feet. Keep the rigid canonical weapon unchanged when planted: clip the buried part at the registered foot plane, mirror the visible seam and retain released hand masks/ground anchors. Native pixel checks and GIFs support inspection but do not substitute for owner feel approval.

## Scope

These conventions apply to Godot 4.x GDScript, scenes, resources, and game assets. Prefer consistency with an established local pattern when it is clearly intentional; record any durable convention change here.

## Naming

- Files and folders: `snake_case`.
- GDScript variables and functions: `snake_case`.
- Classes and named custom resources: `PascalCase` via `class_name` when globally useful.
- Constants and enum members: `UPPER_SNAKE_CASE`.
- Signals: past-tense or state-event `snake_case`, such as `health_changed` or `died`.
- Private implementation members: leading underscore when it improves API clarity.
- Node names: `PascalCase`, descriptive, and stable within reusable scenes.
- Input actions: namespaced `snake_case`, such as `player_move_left` and `player_dodge`.
- Regional vocabulary is scoped content, never the game's global naming template. Forest/root/tree/bark/grove language may be used across planned Stages I-X when a character, creature, material, or place genuinely belongs to that ecology, but do not force the motif into every name. Unrelated Sanctuary characters, cross-region services, generic systems, and Stage XI+ content require personal, neutral, or lore-meaningful naming rather than inheriting the Forest motif by habit.
- Reusable enemies, NPCs, projectiles, VFX, sounds, materials, tiles, and environment modules use identity-owned folders and filenames. Never prefix or nest reusable content under `stage_7`, `stage_9`, or another introduction stage. Stage folders own only layouts, encounter composition, stage-specific flow, and assets that truly cannot exist outside that stage.

## GDScript Organization

Use this order when sections are present:

1. `class_name` and `extends`
2. Documentation comment
3. Signals
4. Enums
5. Constants
6. Exported variables
7. Public variables/properties
8. Private variables
9. `@onready` references
10. Godot lifecycle callbacks
11. Public methods
12. Private methods

Use static typing for public APIs, exported data, signals, return values, and non-obvious local values. Avoid `Variant` unless polymorphism is intentional.

## Script Responsibilities

- Assisted controls may produce movement or action intent, but `Player` remains the only body/action authority. Manual input has explicit priority, selection identifies one actor, and target UI/markers remain observers.
- Hitstop is presentation-only. Enemy interruption requires explicit `DamageInfo.stagger_seconds` plus the target's crowd-control policy; never infer stagger from pause duration or repeated input.
- Configure repeat-interruption limits and resistance windows on `EnemyDefinition`; do not add hit counters to individual enemy controllers. A resistance window may reject gameplay stagger only—accepted damage, flash, audio, particles, and bounded hitstop must remain intact.
- Control tier describes reaction strength, not encounter rank. A recurring normal mob may use `HEAVY` without becoming an Elite/mini-boss, and a boss should use data-owned immunity rather than skill-specific exceptions.

- A script should have one clear reason to change.
- Prefer components and collaborators over deep inheritance trees.
- Avoid giant actor controllers; extract coherent capabilities once boundaries are understood.
- Do not create an abstraction for a single trivial use unless it enforces an important boundary.
- Presentation scripts must not silently own combat rules.
- Hit flash and hitstop may observe only accepted-hit signals. They must remain presentation feedback and must not decide damage or contact. Preserve target-local confirmation, but coalesce camera, hitstop, and impact-audio work once per swing/cast impact so multi-target contacts cannot stack pauses or rebuild shared effects.
- Avoid hidden mutations of shared `Resource` assets.
- Fix an incorrect sprite silhouette in processed frame pixels. Permanent node scale is not an acceptable substitute for correcting generated/native actor scale; temporary authored squash/stretch such as spawning remains presentation-only.

## Signals

- Signals notify that something happened; methods request that something happen.
- Declare typed signal parameters.
- Connect/disconnect dynamic relationships with lifecycle ownership in mind.
- Avoid signal chains that obscure the source of a gameplay state change.
- Do not use a global event bus as the default communication mechanism.

## Error Handling

- Validate external/configuration data at boundaries.
- Use assertions for developer invariants, not recoverable player-facing failures.
- Fail visibly in development when required dependencies are missing.
- Do not silently swallow invalid state.

- Autosave only at authored safe milestones. UI may request Continue/New Journey, but `SaveService` owns file validation, recovery, deletion, and restoration. Headless tests must use isolated `user://` paths or suppress production-path persistence.

Mutable inventories store only known IDs and plain values, never `Resource` instances or display labels. Recipe discovery remains separate from story flags unless a recipe is itself a narrative key item.

## Asset Review Checklist

Before approving a sprite, prop, tile set, or environment scene, verify:

1. Pixel density, palette, lighting direction, and origin match the project baseline.
2. Silhouette and interaction state remain readable during combat.
3. Collision and navigation match the intended traversable footprint.
4. Foreground/background ordering works from every relevant approach direction.
5. The asset is reusable and does not contain unnecessary level-specific coupling.
6. Animation, effects, and shadows do not obscure hazards or player feedback.
