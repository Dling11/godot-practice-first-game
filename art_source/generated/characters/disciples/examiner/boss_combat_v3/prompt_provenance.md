# Examiner Boss Combat V3 Prompt Provenance

- Created: 2026-08-25
- Generator: OpenAI built-in ImageGen
- Identity references: `examiner_compact_pixel_style_lock_v3_2026-08-24.png` and the accepted fresh Precision Thrust board
- Runtime conversion: `tools/process_examiner_animation_sources.py`

Accepted boards use a strict 6-column by 4-row source layout, compact chunky pixel art, the approved masked ivory/charcoal/gold identity, top-down/slightly angled perspective, stable roots, consistent proportions, generous cell separation, and no baked VFX. Each board was prompted around six physical beats:

- Precision Thrust: guard, pullback, aim, committed lunge, contact, recovery.
- Divine Sweep: guard, opposite coil, deepest anticipation, full-body cut, wide follow-through, recovery.
- Judgment Charge: guard, target point, compressed buildup, launch, full-speed piercing travel, hard brake.
- Ground Judgment: guard, lift, high overhead anticipation, downward commitment, planted ground contact, controlled recovery.

Rejected generations that duplicated thrust poses, returned the wrong grid, drifted into realistic detail, or crossed neighboring cells were not copied into the repository.
