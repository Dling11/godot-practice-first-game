## A — SLIM RUNIC

Use case: ui-mockup. Asset type: HP HUD design choice preview for Battle of Gods, a top-down hard-pixel fantasy RPG at 960x540. Match the game's existing art language: charcoal outlines, obsidian/slate, muted aged gold, ivory and restrained violet. Create a new much more compact HP widget replacing the large feathered box concept. Crisp deliberately chunky pixel art, strictly horizontal geometry, no smooth vector gradients or glossy mobile-game plastic. At intended game size this HP-only widget fits about 250x46 logical pixels. Generous uninterrupted readable red health fill and high-contrast ivory numerals. No XP bar, coins, inventory, background scenery or character portrait. Pixel decoration stays at endpoints and never competes with the health information. Present a polished comparison card with exactly TWO copies of the SAME designed widget: top FULL HEALTH showing exact text 'HP 140 / 140' with a full muted crimson bar; bottom LOW HEALTH showing exact text 'HP 28 / 140' with 20% crimson fill and clear exact 'LOW HP' caption near the bar. Empty portion is deep charcoal, with sharp dark outline. Uniform pure very dark blue-charcoal card background. Each widget is enlarged by an integer factor so the pixel craftsmanship is inspectable; abundant space around both, no clipping. Small clean ivory heading at the very top with the requested option name, and tiny subtitles 'FULL HEALTH' and 'LOW HEALTH' above respective examples. No feather wings, sprawling branches, large orb, giant shield, spiky horns or ornamental box containing irrelevant stats. This is a concept preview only, not claimed as playable UI. Heading (verbatim): 'A — SLIM RUNIC'. Design A: a very slim flat rectangular health strip, charcoal one-pixel outline, thin worn-gold lip and tiny angular violet rune tabs at the two ends. The HP numerals sit directly above the strip on a small dark inset. Open lightweight silhouette, maximum visible health, almost no ornament. This should be the cleanest and most practical option.
