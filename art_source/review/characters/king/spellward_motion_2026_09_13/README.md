# Selected C / Spellward — clarity and motion proof

The owner selected appearance option 3 (C Spellward) and requested clearer small-scale art without realism. Spellward is an appearance label, not a gameplay class. This review keeps the compact human silhouette and explores finer pixel detail at the same intended on-screen height.

Open index.html for idle, walk and swing drafts with pause, speed, frame stepping and magnification. Four idle, eight walk and twelve swing source poses were generated using built-in imagegen with the selected C board and then its idle as identity references. Source artwork and full prompts are preserved here. FFmpeg assembles the review GIFs; export.ps1 and registration.json record extraction bounds and one fixed scale per action family. No runtime sprites, collision, movement, skill timing, saves or equipment rules changed.

## Scale and review limitations

- The review samples the body at about 56 image pixels, intended to occupy 28 logical pixels at the game's 2x display. This explores finer source density rather than increasing King's world-space height. A runtime rendering/pixel-snap integration has not been implemented or verified.
- These are one-facing motion studies, not complete production sprite sheets or a recorded game capture. Source boards are not exact runtime atlases; wider action extraction retains weapon tips. Background and edge normalization remain production work.
- Idle keeps a calm shoulder carry with subtle breathing/scarf movement. Swing shows preparation, contact, follow-through and recovery without VFX hiding the pose.
- Walking remains UNAPPROVED: the generator varied stride/arm poses but repeated the lead-leg arrangement too closely in the second half. Two targeted attempts did not establish a convincing opposite-foot contact. The walking draft is shown honestly for review and must not replace production locomotion.
- Generated body/sword registration still needs an animation cleanup pass, including checking action transitions and adjacent-source fragments. These drafts establish design/detail direction, not final motion quality.
- Both GIF sizes decode successfully. Representative idle, walk and contact frames were visually inspected. Selection of C is confirmed; motion and final pixel density await review.

Files ending _size.gif show approximately the game's 2x display body height; _review.gif provides 3x enlargement of those samples. GIFs are silent, and timing is for presentation review only.
