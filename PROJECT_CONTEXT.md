## Durable Boundaries

- `HealthComponent`, hitboxes, hurtboxes, actor controllers, and encounter services own gameplay authority.
- Animation, HUD, effects, and audio observe gameplay; they do not calculate authoritative damage or state.
- Prefer composition and data-driven resources over deep inheritance.
- Future materials, drop profiles, loot tables, recipes, and regional unlocks must be stable-ID data resources. Mutable ownership belongs to dedicated inventory/crafting/save authorities; enemies and UI may request or observe outcomes but must not own loot rolls, recipes, balances, or saved state.
- Reuse material art through documented container/silhouette templates, then export distinct flattened runtime PNGs. Labels, rarity frames, and gameplay meaning remain UI/data responsibilities; palette alone must never distinguish dangerous enemies or materially different drops.
- Environment props must align presentation, collision, navigation, depth, and occlusion.
- Combat-stage ground must come from baked authored TileMap cells backed by reusable layout resources and organized shared/region TileSets; do not restore runtime-random terrain fill.
- Use signals, timers, and state transitions instead of avoidable per-frame polling.
- Preserve King's stable anatomy, center/pivot, and foot baseline across actions. Use `AnimatedSprite2D`/named `SpriteFrames`, approve one direction/action family at gameplay scale, keep oversized VFX separate, require binary alpha/matte cleanup, and keep contact shapes data-owned. Archived Opaw dimensions are historical reference only and no longer constrain production.
